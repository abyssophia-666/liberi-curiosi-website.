THE LIBERI CURIOSI PHILOSOPHY

“If you have not learned anything today, did you really live?”

— C. Zaparolli

We believe education should be something we delight in; something that awakens curiosity not only within our children, but within ourselves. The ability to learn, to question, to explore, and to pursue knowledge is an extraordinary privilege, one easily taken for granted, yet one that many throughout the world can only wish to have.

Liberi Curiosi exists to honor that privilege by helping families cultivate curiosity, independence, scholarship, and a lifelong love of learning. Education should not simply be something we complete, but something we carry with us throughout our lives. As my father used to say, “If you have not learned anything today, did you really live?”

Our tools are designed around the family rather than the institution: flexible enough for real life, thoughtful enough for serious study, and beautiful enough to make learning feel like an invitation rather than an obligation.

THE COMMUNITY

Collegium

A gathering place for homeschool co-ops, microschools, learning circles, and families seeking meaningful community. Classes and learning opportunities are designed for parents as well as children, making education something the whole family can share. Families can arrange playdates, create safe child profiles to showcase work and accomplishments, and connect through parent-controlled pen pals—giving children space to form friendships while parents remain in control of their privacy and connections.
